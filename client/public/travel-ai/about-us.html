About Travel AI

A small team, two destinations, one idea.

We believe that planning a holiday should feel like a meaningful conversation. By combining deep human expertise with specialized AI systems, we coordinate itineraries that honor your time and pace.

Where this started

We got tired of planning trips badly.

Travel AI was born in Auckland, New Zealand in 2023. Our founders wanted to solve a basic paradox: B2B booking tools were cold and algorithmic, while traditional agents were slow and expensive.

We realized that if we built specialized AI to handle the complex routing, database matching, and real-time transit checking, our human agents could focus entirely on what they do best: applying taste, empathy, and real experience.

We purposefully limited our practice to just two places—Japan and Dubai. This singular focus ensures we hold genuine, up-to-date depth in the areas we manage.

Our principles

Four things we won't compromise on.

  • A person, always

    Software can suggest. People decide. We leverage AI to handle data density, but every final journey is shaped and signed off by a human who knows the destination.

  • Depth over breadth

    Two places, known well. We only plan journeys to Japan and Dubai. We don't branch out, and we don't scale globally, because authentic deep knowledge can't be rushed.

  • Your pace

    No pressure, no countdown timers. Holidays are about space and stillness. We structure itineraries with deliberate blank spaces to let serendipity happen.

  • Straight answers

    We'll tell you when it's a bad idea. If a route is too ambitious, a neighbourhood too noisy, or a season too humid, we'll give you clear, unvarnished truth.

Behind the journeys

Every itinerary is written by someone who has been there.

Our agents are not just booking processors. They are deep local specialists who spend months every year on the ground in Kyoto, Shinjuku, or the Dubai desert.

When you consult with us, our proprietary Travel AI platform structures the core logistics instantly—flight routes, rail timetables, and room availability. This allows your human advisor to spend their energy tailoring the fine details.

The result is a highly tailored, stress-free holiday managed continuously via our companion app. No automated templates, just human taste accelerated by technology.
