Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you create an account, book travel, or contact support. This may include personal details (name, email, phone), payment information, and travel preferences. We also collect usage data (device, browser, IP, interactions) and cookie data to understand how you use the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the service, personalize recommendations, communicate with you about your trips and account, and detect fraud. We may also use aggregated data to improve destination suggestions and travel planning features.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We share data with third-party services (payment processors, travel providers) to facilitate bookings and support. We may also disclose information to comply with legal requirements, protect our rights, or respond to a request from law enforcement.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security safeguards, including encryption and access controls, to protect your information. While we take steps to secure your data, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You can also opt out of marketing communications and manage cookie preferences. Some choices may affect your ability to use certain features.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, authenticate sessions, and analyze usage. You can manage cookie settings in your browser, but some features may not function as intended.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. International Data Transfers

We may transfer data across borders to operate the service globally. When we do, we apply appropriate safeguards to protect your information in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

9. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. After that period, we securely delete or de-identify your data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice through the service. Your continued use after changes become effective means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact support@travelai.com. We are happy to help with any inquiries regarding your data and privacy.
